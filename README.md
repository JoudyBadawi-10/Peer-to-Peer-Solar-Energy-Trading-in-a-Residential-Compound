#peer to peer solar energy trading in a residential compound

#Overview 
This project aims to make a system that allows residents of the compound to trade solar energy together with price better than the grid for both sides.
As villas have rooftop solar panels and home batteries. On sunny days they produce more electricity than they use,
while apartment residents buy everything from the national grid.
Our villa owners asked: why can't we sell our extra power to our neighbors?
so, this is what we are trying to solve.
